How I build

01

Native, not wrapped

Built with each platform’s own tools rather than a web view in a costume. Apps launch instantly, respect your system settings, and behave like everything else on the device because they are made the same way.

02

Your data stays yours

Work happens on your device wherever it can. No account to create, nothing uploaded that does not need to be, and no analytics watching what you do.

03

Bought once

A price, not a subscription. You keep the version you paid for, and updates come for as long as the app is alive.
