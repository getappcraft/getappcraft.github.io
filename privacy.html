Privacy

Last updated 22 September 2026

Short version: Debrief does not collect anything about you, and I do not operate a server that your videos touch.

What the app does with your videos

Everything happens on your Mac. Transcription, screen capture and summarisation all run using Apple’s on-device models. Your recordings, transcripts and the folders Debrief produces stay on your disk and are never sent anywhere.

If you open a video from a URL, Debrief fetches it from that address and nowhere else. If you save a ClickUp API token, it is stored in your macOS Keychain and only ever sent to ClickUp.

What the app sends

  • Licence checks. When you activate or verify your licence, the licence key and an anonymous identifier derived from your Mac’s hardware ID are sent to Lemon Squeezy, who handle licensing. The identifier is a one-way hash; it cannot be reversed into anything about you or your machine.
  • Update checks. The app asks my update feed whether a newer version exists. This is an ordinary web request and includes no identifying information beyond your current version.

There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry and no account.

What happens when you buy

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, who act as the seller of record. They receive your email and payment details in order to take the payment, issue a receipt and send your licence key. I can see your email address and order details in their dashboard; I never see your card details. Their privacy policy governs that transaction.
