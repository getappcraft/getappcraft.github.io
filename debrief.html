What your agent actually reads

A plain folder. No database, no proprietary format — just files, laid out so an agent spends tokens on the summary and only opens a screenshot when the summary says to.

  • manifest.md The entry point. Summary, every request as a table, and a one-line description of each screenshot.
  • ai/ The cleaned transcript with misheard product names corrected, the requests as JSON, and the frames worth looking at.
  • raw/ Everything before processing, for when the summary is not enough.
  • marks/ The exact frames you flagged while watching.

Questions

Can I try it first?

Yes. The first three videos you process are free, with nothing held back — full transcript, screens and summary. The licence prompt appears on the fourth. The trial is counted in sessions rather than days because the product is judged per video, not per week.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Transcription and summarisation run on your Mac using Apple’s on-device models. There is no account, no server of mine, and no analytics. A video opened from a URL is fetched from that URL and nowhere else.

Which videos work?

Local files in any format macOS plays, and direct video links — including ClickUp attachments. YouTube, Loom and Vimeo links do not work: those sites do not serve a video file a player can open.

How good is the summary, honestly?

It depends on the recording. Clear narration over an app produces a genuinely useful list of requests. Rambling, heavy background noise, or unusual product names can produce something rougher — which is why the verbatim transcript and every captured frame are always kept alongside it, and why you get three free sessions to judge it on your own videos.

How many Macs?

Three. Enough for a laptop, a desktop and a spare, and you can move a licence between machines from Settings.

What if it does not work for me?

Email me and I will refund you. The three free sessions exist so this should rarely be necessary — please check the requirements above before buying, since Debrief cannot run without Apple Intelligence.
